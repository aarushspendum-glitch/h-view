How It Works

Four steps from install to insight.

No wiring, no downtime, no guesswork — just a sensor that learns your equipment and tells you when something changes.

01

Stick-on install

We mount a small sensor box directly onto your HVAC unit. Nothing to bolt, cut, or rewire — done in minutes, with zero downtime.

02

Connects over WiFi

An onboard ESP32 with an accelerometer and gyroscope streams real vibration and motion data live over your WiFi network.

03

Learns your baseline

For the first 24 hours, H-VIEW quietly calibrates — learning what "idle" and "active" normally look like for your specific unit.

04

Live on your dashboard

From then on, you get real-time readings and a clear signal the moment vibration drifts away from your unit's normal baseline.

Built For

Designed around rooftop RTUs — adaptable to almost anything else.

Rooftop units are our main focus: exposed, hard to keep an eye on, and expensive to let fail quietly. But H-VIEW's sensor and calibration are built to be configured per install, so we tune the setup to whatever HVAC system your business actually runs.
