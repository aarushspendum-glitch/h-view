Four steps from install to insight.

SEC. 01 / HOW IT WORKS
STEP / 01

Stick‑on install

We mount a small sensor box directly on your unit. Nothing bolted, cut, or rewired — done in minutes, no downtime.

STEP / 02

Connects over WiFi

An onboard ESP32 with an accelerometer and gyroscope streams real vibration and motion data over your network.

STEP / 03

Learns your baseline

For the first 7 days, H‑VIEW quietly calibrates — learning what idle and active look like for your unit, across a full weekly cycle.

STEP / 04

Live on your dashboard

From there, real‑time readings and a clear signal the moment vibration drifts from normal.

SEC. 02 / BUILT FOR

Designed around rooftop RTUs.

Rooftop units are our main focus: exposed, hard to keep an eye on, and expensive to let fail quietly. The sensor and calibration are configured per install, so we tune the setup to whatever system your business runs.
